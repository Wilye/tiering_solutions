run with scoring inversion

findings:
- no migrations happening with inverted sort because CBA is filtering them out. it sees that the "hottest" page in the
scores array is way colder than the "coldest" page in the scores array, so it doesn't choose to migrate anything.
- the initial placement of pages happen to be optimal enough that it doesn't affect anything for XSBench
- there are no hotness score violations because the boundary calculated (dramsize / page_size) is shifted too far up
in the scores array. at 1:8 ratio, DRAM holds 1/9th of pages. so boundary is ~3,600 of ~32,000 pages for XSBench.
the 3,600 pages are cold. warm pages don't appear until later.
